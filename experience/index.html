Technical Product Owner (TPO)
PT. MAPCLUB Digital Asia • Omnichannel Retail Loyalty
2026 — Present (Active)

Powering omnichannel retail loyalty and digital engagement for hundreds of international brands across Indonesia. Replacing fragile manual workflows with automated, verified enterprise software.

The Operational Friction
Marketing campaigns and corporate finance were separated by dozens of disconnected Excel workbooks. Verifying campaign billing took weeks of manual cross-checking with high error risk.
The Engineering Architecture
Engineered an automated Marketing Services invoicing pipeline. Instituted strict soft API contracts, deterministic MoSCoW scoping, and zero-defect verification gates across squads.
The Measurable Impact
Eliminated manual billing bottlenecks, secured C-suite executive sign-off, and accelerated specification handoffs from weeks to hours.
Product Manager (Flight Refunds & Disruptions)
Traveloka • High-Scale Aviation Systems
2024 — 2026

When flights are canceled or delayed, passengers experience extreme anxiety while airlines operate on cryptic fare rules and strict government regulations (PM 185/2015).

The Operational Friction
Airline delay policies varied wildly across 10+ flight provider APIs. Manual calculation discrepancies caused customer disputes and delayed refund payouts for days.
The Engineering Architecture
Overhauled the calculation engine accuracy from 87.9% to 94.2% using intelligent fare-rule parsing algorithms, automated tax reconciliation, and instant customer status updates.
The Measurable Impact
Doubled automated resolutions (16.8% → 34.4%), slashed customer dispute complaints by 50%, and unlocked ~Rp10 Billion in net business revenue in 3 months.
Project Specialist (Heavy Industry & Energy)
Boston Consulting Group (BCG) • Industrial Mega-Projects
2023 — 2024

Deploying industrial engineering on the ground: wearing hard hats and steel-toed boots in nickel mining, smelters, and geothermal energy sites across Indonesia.

The Operational Friction

The Engineering Architecture
Structured 10+ commercial engineering tenders and modeled full lifecycle Operations & Maintenance (O&M) schedules grounded in plant-floor realities.
The Measurable Impact
Advanced 30% of competitive bids to final contract awards, bridging the gap between boardroom financial targets and physical machinery.
