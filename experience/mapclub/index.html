system architecture 01 [ ai-assisted product operations workspace ]
the bottleneck: 1-week turnaround
Drafting comprehensive business requirements documents (BRDs), system flowcharts, and interactive prototypes manually took approximately one week per initiative.
what i built: workspace ownership & results
Built an AI-assisted Product Operations workspace myself that turns a single prompt into a structured business requirements document (BRD), process diagram, and interactive prototype. Stated result: reduced BRD preparation time from approximately one week to one day or less.
human judgment & verification workflow
The workflow begins from a single prompt but relies on explicit clarification steps, edge-case review, and human evaluation. AI assists drafting; product judgment verifies scope boundaries, business logic, and acceptance criteria before engineering handoff.
concrete security & data safeguards
Implemented verifiable security controls: passcode verification with signed cookies, strict synthetic-data rules forbidding real customer or transaction records, and automated pre-commit scanning scripts.
system architecture 02 [ ticketing services & checkout experience ]
ticketing services dashboard
Owned requirements for a Ticketing Services dashboard covering campaign requests, delivery execution, and partner invoicing. Translated complex cross-departmental operations into actionable specifications, securing C-level approval for the proposed solution.
checkout coupon application & delivery addresses
Defined improvements to coupon application at checkout and delivery-address entry, translating customer problems into clear requirements and testable prototypes.

mapclub app revamp coordination

specification rigor & acceptance sign-offs
Standardized business requirements document (BRD) structures and Berita Acara Acceptance (BAC) sign-offs, coordinating edge-case review and payment testing across engineering teams.
