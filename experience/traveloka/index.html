system architecture 01 [ commercial product & revenue logic ]
modified inventory ticket product
Owned end-to-end development of a modified inventory ticket product, redesigning marked-up refund fee policies and discounted pricing logic. Maintained strict compliance with Indonesian Ministry of Transportation PM 185 tahun 2015.
commercial outcome: ~idr 10bn net revenue
Generated approximately IDR 10 billion in net revenue within 3 months of launch from a modified inventory ticket product with redesigned refund-fee policies and discounted pricing logic.
system architecture 02 [ calculation accuracy & provider api integration ]
tax & commission calculation engine
Led direct provider API integration and engineered a calculation engine supporting new tax components (VAT, service charges) and commissions, significantly reducing customer and operational escalations.
accuracy improvement: 87.97% to 94.24%
Improved flight refund calculation accuracy from 87.97% to 94.24% by leading direct provider API integration and engineering a tax (VAT, service charges) and commission calculation engine.
system architecture 03 [ operations automation & fare rule interpretation ]
rpa refund closing automation: 16.76% to 34.38%
Doubled refund closing automation from 16.76% to 34.38% through robotic process automation (RPA), cutting manual handling time and freeing operations capacity.
policy completeness (95.76%) & cat 16 fare rules
Drove refund policy completeness to 95.76% via direct provider API integration and built an anomaly detection dashboard to flag illogical policy data for provider escalation. Improved an LLM-powered workflow to parse and translate airline CAT 16 Fare Rules in real-time, designed to automate refund policy interpretation at scale.
