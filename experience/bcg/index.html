bridging boardroom financials with machine physics

In multi-hundred-million-dollar industrial projects, spreadsheet financial models often detach from physical realities on the ground. When turnaround schedules, heat rate losses, or equipment degradation curves are miscalculated, balance sheets face massive write-downs. My mandate at BCG was developing grounded engineering-economic models that linked boardroom capital allocation to thermodynamic and operational reliability.

domain deep dive 01 [ $400m industrial proposals & cost estimation ]
the challenge
Evaluating complex engineering proposals across greenfield sites with high geographic friction, unhedged equipment procurement lead times, and uncertain operational ramp-up periods.
what i built
Built dynamic Capex/Opex parametric valuation models connecting equipment procurement timelines, material cost escalations, and yield curves to stress-test project returns across diverse commodity price scenarios.
domain deep dive 02 [ nickel smelting & geothermal power sites ]
nickel smelter turnaround maintenance
Modeled maintenance overhaul schedules and furnace relining shutdowns. Optimized spare part supply chain buffers to minimize costly furnace idle periods while maintaining strict worker safety thresholds.
geothermal power plant reliability
Analyzed wellhead steam output decline rates, turbine heat-rate degradation, and cooling loop thermodynamic efficiency to structure multi-year asset maintenance strategies that protect baseline power generation.
