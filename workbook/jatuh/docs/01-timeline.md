## 3. the main path and its failure modes

### main path

1. **4 dec 2026:** submit to RCA round 1 (gate G2 passed). hard stop 18 dec.
2. **mid jan 2027:** IELTS. result in days.
3. **late jan to feb 2027:** LPDP tahap 1 registration, with the IELTS result, and the RCA offer if it is in and counts as an LoA.
4. **by 19 feb:** RCA decision. accept within 3 weeks.
5. **may to jul 2027:** LPDP substance and final result. **sept 2027:** start.

### failure modes

| # | what fails | when you know | fallback | cost |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| F1 | IELTS slot or score not ready by early february | early feb | take the LPDP **tahap 2** path (F5). RCA is unaffected; its english deadline is 1 jun | the 2027 start is at risk |
| F2 | IELTS score below 6.5 overall or 5.5 in a skill | days after the test | retake within 2 to 3 weeks (computer delivery makes this quick). still inside tahap 1 if the first test is in mid january | about Rp 3.5 to 4 juta per retake |
| F3 | no RCA decision before tahap 1 closes | late feb | register for tahap 1 without an LoA **if the 2027 guide allows it for S2 abroad**. if an LoA is mandatory: tahap 2 (F5) | depends on the 2027 rules |
| F4 | RCA round 1 says no | by 19 feb | round 2 (23 mar) with v3 and three play-tests; decision by 5 may. LPDP tahap 1 continues if registered without an LoA | a later offer |
| F5 | LPDP tahap 1 missed or rejected | feb to jul | **tahap 2** (registration about 30 jun to 31 jul 2027, result about november 2027). ask RCA to **defer** the place to sept 2028. if deferral isn't allowed: reapply for 2028 entry in round 1 (oct 2027 to jan 2028) with a stronger portfolio | a one-year delay, but not a loss |
| F6 | RCA deposit due before any LPDP decision | 3 weeks after the offer | decide now whether you would pay it yourself. check the amount and refund terms | the deposit, if LPDP later says no |
| F7 | rules or eligibility unclear (LoA wording, accepted tests, RCA on the target list, limits on reapplying after a rejection) | when the 2027 guide is out | do not plan around guesses. check each point in the guide, or ask LPDP directly | none if checked early |
