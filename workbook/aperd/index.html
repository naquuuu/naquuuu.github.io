section 01

ecosystem actor map & three-rail separation

the foundation of mutual fund architecture is the strict segregation of three distinct ledgers: cash (escrow and custody accounts), units (ksei s-invest fractional balances), and instructions (aperd oms orders).

under ojk pojk 39/2014, the aperd platform acts purely as an order broker and distributor. the aperd platform never touches investor cash or mutual fund units.

figure 1. actor map with 3 colored rails: money (sage), units (sky), instructions (terra)
💵
aperd holds zero cash
investor money flows directly to segregated bank kustodian fund accounts. overnight float retention is illegal under pojk 39/2014.
🏛️
ksei owns the ledger
the platform database is merely an advisory mirror. legal beneficial ownership exists only as recorded in s-invest.
📑
direct redemption payout
bank kustodian wires redemption proceeds directly to the investor's verified bank account. aperd never touches liquidation proceeds.
section 02

the national clearing clock: from order intake to t+7 payout

mutual fund clearing operates on a rigid national timetable governed by exchange days (hari bursa). missing the 13:00 wib cut-off by even one second rolls order pricing by an entire exchange day.

figure 2. national daily clearing timetable with the 13:00 wib cut-off line highlighted
📅
hari bursa convention
indonesia stock exchange trading days only (mon-fri minus public holidays). weekend orders price at monday's closing nav.
⚡
13:00 hard ceiling
both order and payment must clear before 13:00 wib. post-cutoff orders legally roll to next exchange day (nab t+1).
🛡️
t+7 statutory maximum
custodian bank must wire redemption funds within 7 exchange days. money market funds typically disburse in t+1 to t+2.
section 03

good order × good funds: the 2×2 cut-off decision matrix

an investor receives today's closing nav (nab day t) if and only if both independent conditions are satisfied before 13:00 wib. if either leg arrives late, pricing rolls over automatically.

t+0 nav (same-day execution)

good order ≤ 13:00 × good funds ≤ 13:00

both order instruction in s-invest and cash in bank kustodian custody account arrived prior to cut-off. order is allotted at day t closing nav.

t+1 nav (rollover to next day)

good order ≤ 13:00 × good funds > 13:00 (late cash)

investor paid at 12:56 wib via aggregator, but bank sweep landed at bank kustodian at 13:04 wib. funds stamped post cut-off; order prices at nab t+1.

t+1 nav (rollover to next day)

good order > 13:00 × good funds ≤ 13:00 (late order)

funds arrived in escrow early, but user submitted order instruction at 13:02 wib. order misses cut-off and rolls over to nab t+1.

rejected / refund suspense

order missing / third-party payer name mismatch

cash received from bank account whose name does not match investor ktp/sid. rejected under apu-ppt rules; frozen in suspense for manual refund.

⏱️
why aggregator vas need a 12:30 buffer
interbank clearing between an aggregator and the custodian bank takes 15-30 minutes. platforms set a 12:30 internal cut-off buffer to guarantee funds sweep before 13:00 wib.
🆔
sid & ifua multi-goal architecture
one master sid per investor, but separate sub-ifuas per in-app goal (e.g. emergency fund vs wedding) to prevent pooled fifo cost-basis distortion at ksei aksed.
section 04

order state machine & forward pricing visual

mutual funds execute blind under forward pricing: when you place an order, you see yesterday's price, but you receive today's closing price calculated after market close.

🧮
forward pricing worked math card
you invest: Rp 1,000,000 (display price: Rp 1,200.0000 yesterday)
market closes at 16:00 WIB → Bank Kustodian calculates closing NAB: Rp 1,250.0000
units allotted at T+1: Rp 1,000,000 / 1,250.0000 = 800.0000 UP (exact 4-decimal precision)
figure 3. order lifecycle state machine with green-toned happy path and rose failure branches
📥
subscription (nominal in)
investor specifies rupiah amount (e.g. Rp 10,000,000). received units are rounded to 4 decimals at closing nav.
📤
redemption (units out)
investor specifies units to liquidate. bank kustodian wires cash directly to investor minus interbank fee (bi-fast Rp 2,500).
🔀
switching constraints
native s-invest switch only within same mi. cross-mi switch requires 2-leg chained machine via rdn with 2-day market slippage.
section 05

payment integration & 4-gate webhook safety strip

treating a payment webhook as an entitlement instruction without verification is the most common cause of fintech loss. every payment notification must pass through a strict 4-gate checkpoint strip:

figure 4. 4-gate webhook safety strip: signature check, status poll, dedupe keys, and monotonic rank law
🔑
K_charge: attempt dedupe
prevents minting duplicate virtual accounts for the same bill. format: INV-YYYYMMDD-XXXXXX-aN.
🛡️
K_delivery: replay block
unique index on sha256 of raw webhook bytes. identical transport retries result in harmless 200 OK no-ops.
💰
K_settle: cash credit dedupe
primary key on payment facts ledger. ensures a single payment receipt can never credit internal cash ledgers twice.
📈
K_allot: unit allotment dedupe
unique index on s-invest subscription instructions. guarantees that paying once can never allot mutual fund units twice.

section 07

the 8 critical domain gaps: fixed-price commerce vs. mutual funds

here are the 8 architectural patterns that exist in regulated wealthtech platforms that never exist in standard e-commerce or ticketing:

1️⃣
forward pricing & 4-decimal math
what it is: execution price (closing nab t) is unknown at checkout. subscription is nominal idr in; redemption is 4-decimal units out.
why it bites: partial nominal redemptions breach minimum holding balance if nab drops afternoon.
2️⃣
dual-clock 13:00 wib cut-off
what it is: both good order (s-invest) and good funds (bank kustodian) must clear before 13:00 wib.
why it bites: payment gateway webhook saying paid at 12:57 wib still misses cut-off if interbank sweep arrives at 13:04 wib.
3️⃣
s-invest batch cycles & multi-ifua
what it is: national daily batch timetable (08:00-13:00 intake, 17:00-21:00 nav upload, 10:00-14:00 t+1 allotment).
why it bites: separate in-app portfolio goals require dedicated sub-ifuas to prevent pooled fifo cost-basis distortion.
4️⃣
native vs synthetic cross-mi switch
what it is: native s-invest switch works only within same mi. cross-mi switch requires a 2-leg chained machine via rdn.
why it bites: client experiences 2 days of market price exposure while money moves between custodians.
5️⃣
bank kustodian redemption payout
what it is: aperd never touches redemption cash; custodian bank wires proceeds directly to client bank account.
why it bites: bank transfer fee (bi-fast Rp 2,500) deducted from payout creates support tickets if not previewed in ui.
6️⃣
daily nav fee accrual & 0% tax
what it is: management fees are accrued daily out of fund assets prior to publishing nab; returns are 0% pph tax under uu pph.
why it bites: aperd earns revenue via monthly trailer fee sharing invoiced to the mi based on settled aum.
7️⃣
corporate actions (phi dividends)
what it is: fixed-income funds distribute yields as cash or automatic unit reinvestments without user-placed orders.
why it bites: ignoring corporate action files in s-invest ingestion causes client balances to drift lower than ksei aksed (xu-12).
8️⃣
suitability mismatch gates (seojk 51)
what it is: client risk score (1-5) mapped to fund risk. high-risk fund triggers a blocking mismatch warning modal.
why it bites: failing to capture immutable electronic consent logs creates severe ojk mis-selling sanctions.
section 08

5 rapid self-test questions (60-second interview drills)

click each question to reveal the concise, high-rigor architectural answer:

q1: why cannot an investor buy an exact unit count, and why might a partial nominal redemption fail at 17:00 wib?
answer key:
• forward pricing: transaction price (closing NAB Day T) is unknown at checkout and published only after market close by the Bank Kustodian. orders must be nominal-driven (IDR in, 4-decimal units out).
• minimum balance breach: if an investor redeems nominal Rupiah leaving a narrow margin above the prospectus-mandated Saldo Minimum Kepemilikan (e.g. Rp 100,000), an afternoon market drop will lower remaining unit value below the threshold.
• architectural fix: enforce a 10% volatility buffer or mandate full liquidation ("Jual Semua Unit").
q2: an investor pays a virtual account at 12:56 wib and gateway says paid at 12:57 wib. do they get day t nav?
answer key:
• not guaranteed: receiving Day T NAV requires both Good Order (S-INVEST) and Good Funds (cleared in Bank Kustodian custody account) prior to 13:00 WIB.
• aggregator transit delay: if funds land in the Bank Kustodian account at 13:04 WIB via interbank sweep, the Custodian legally stamps funds as post cut-off.
• consequence: the order rolls over to receive Day T+1 NAV. direct BK Virtual Accounts or RDN debits eliminate this sweep latency.
q3: can an investor switch directly from a mandiri money market fund to a schroder equity fund in one instruction?
answer key:
• no: native S-INVEST switching instructions are strictly atomic within the same Manajer Investasi.
• synthetic cross-mi machine: the APERD platform must orchestrate a 2-leg chained machine: Leg 1 redeems Fund A to client's RDN at T+2; Leg 2 auto-subscribes Fund B upon RDN credit webhook.
• trade-off: the investor experiences 2 days of market price exposure while money clears between custodian banks.
q4: what happens if an investor edits their bank account in the app and immediately clicks redeem?
answer key:
• redemption must be blocked: the platform must hold the redemption until the bank account amendment is successfully acknowledged by KSEI S-INVEST.
• custodian wires to s-invest record: the Bank Kustodian disburses cash directly to the bank account on file in S-INVEST, not the APERD local database.
• failure mode: un-synchronized amendments cause the Custodian to wire funds to the old closed bank account, resulting in a bank bounce/retur.
q5: how does daily 3-way reconciliation work, what are the three sources, and what are the two most critical breaks?
answer key:
• three sources: (A) APERD OMS (commercial intent), (B) Bank Custody Statement (cash authority net of MDR), (C) KSEI S-INVEST Allotment File (unit authority).
• pairwise passes: A↔B for cash, A↔C for units, B↔C for orphan residual sweep.
• two most critical S1 breaks:
1. (1, 1, 0) XU-01: paid order with cleared cash but zero S-INVEST units (primary client harm).
2. (1, 0, 1) XU-02 / XC-01: units allotted without cleared cash (severe regulatory/custody breach).
