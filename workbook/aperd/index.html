Who's Who & Accounts #aperd

APERD (Agen Penjual Efek Reksa Dana)

🟢 In Plain Words (What It Really Is)

The licensed mutual fund store/platform (like Bibit, Bareksa, or a bank's wealth app) that lets users buy and sell mutual funds.

🔵 System Analogy (How It Maps to What You Built)

Like Traveloka for flights: Traveloka sells the ticket and collects the order, but does not fly the plane.

🔴 How to Say It in the Interview

“As an APERD platform under POJK 39/2014, our system is an order router and suitability gate - we never hold investor cash or manage the fund portfolio.”

🇮🇩 Definisi & Kalimat Wawancara (Bahasa Indonesia)

Pengertian: Agen Penjual Efek Reksa Dana - platform berizin OJK tempat pemodal membeli dan menjual reksa dana.

Jawaban Wawancara: “Sebagai platform APERD berizin POJK 39/2014, sistem kami bertindak sebagai router instruksi pesanan dan penyaring profil risiko (suitability gate). Sistem kami tidak mengendapkan dana nasabah di rekening penampungan internal (float Rp 0) karena dana pemodal wajib langsung disetorkan ke rekening reksa dana di Bank Kustodian.”

Who's Who & Accounts #mi

Manajer Investasi (MI / Fund Manager)

🟢 In Plain Words (What It Really Is)

The licensed investment company (e.g., Schroders, Mandiri Manajemen Investasi) that decides which stocks, bonds, or deposits the mutual fund buys.

🔵 System Analogy (How It Maps to What You Built)

Like the airline carrier (Garuda/Citilink) that actually operates the flight and sets the product rules.

🔴 How to Say It in the Interview

“Each Manajer Investasi defines fund-level rules in the Prospectus - such as minimum subscription, fee calculation mode, and whether native switching is allowed.”

🇮🇩 Definisi & Kalimat Wawancara (Bahasa Indonesia)

Pengertian: Perusahaan sekuritas/investasi pengelola dana portofolio yang menentukan alokasi aset saham, obligasi, atau pasar uang.

Jawaban Wawancara: “Setiap Manajer Investasi menetapkan aturan transaksi pada Prospektus - seperti batas minimum pembelian, formula biaya langganan (subscription fee), serta apakah pengalihan (switching) antar-produk dalam satu MI didukung secara atomik di KSEI S-INVEST.”

Who's Who & Accounts #bk

Bank Kustodian (BK / Custodian Bank)

🟢 In Plain Words (What It Really Is)

An independent bank (e.g., BCA, Standard Chartered, HSBC) that safely holds all the mutual fund's money and assets, calculates the daily unit price (NAB/UP), and wires cash back to investors when they sell.

🔵 System Analogy (How It Maps to What You Built)

Like an independent bank vault + accounting engine that neither the app nor the fund manager can steal from.

🔴 How to Say It in the Interview

“The Bank Kustodian is the cash authority for Good Funds before 13:00 WIB and calculates the official closing NAB/UP every evening.”

🇮🇩 Definisi & Kalimat Wawancara (Bahasa Indonesia)

Pengertian: Bank umum yang memegang izin penitipan dan pencatatan aset independen, memisahkan uang pemodal dari APERD dan MI.

Jawaban Wawancara: “Bank Kustodian memegang otoritas kas dan penilaian portofolio. Seluruh dana pembelian pemodal ditransfer langsung ke rekening Bank Kustodian, dan dana pencairan (redemption) ditransfer langsung oleh Bank Kustodian ke rekening bank pemodal paling lambat T+7 hari bursa.”

Who's Who & Accounts #sinvest

KSEI S-INVEST

🟢 In Plain Words (What It Really Is)

Indonesia's mandatory national database and clearinghouse (run by KSEI) that connects all APERD apps, Fund Managers (MI), and Custodian Banks (BK). It is the single source of truth for who owns how many mutual fund units.

🔵 System Analogy (How It Maps to What You Built)

Like the central airline GDS (Amadeus/Sabre) or national land registry - if a unit isn't recorded in S-INVEST, the investor doesn't legally own it.

🔴 How to Say It in the Interview

“Our local database is only a mirror; KSEI S-INVEST is the authoritative unit ledger of record that we reconcile against every day.”

🇮🇩 Definisi & Kalimat Wawancara (Bahasa Indonesia)

Pengertian: Sistem Pengelolaan Investasi Terpadu KSEI - hub transaksi dan settlement reksa dana nasional.

Jawaban Wawancara: “S-INVEST adalah sistem sentral yang memproses routing pesanan, pembuatan rekening IFUA, alokasi unit penyertaan harian, dan instruksi pengalihan (switching) secara elektronik di seluruh industri pasar modal Indonesia.”

Who's Who & Accounts #sid

SID (Single Investor Identification)

🟢 In Plain Words (What It Really Is)

A 15-character master investor ID issued by KSEI (e.g., IDD1204AB892104). Every Indonesian citizen (1 NIK) gets only ONE master SID across all investment apps.

🔵 System Analogy (How It Maps to What You Built)

Like a national tax ID (NPWP) or passport number for the capital market.

🔴 How to Say It in the Interview

“During onboarding, we submit the investor's verified e-KYC data to KSEI S-INVEST to provision or link their 15-character master SID before unlocking checkout.”

🇮🇩 Definisi & Kalimat Wawancara (Bahasa Indonesia)

Pengertian: 15 karakter nomor identitas tunggal pemodal di pasar modal Indonesia (berlaku seumur hidup).

Jawaban Wawancara: “Sebelum pemodal dapat bertransaksi reksa dana, sistem kami wajib melakukan verifikasi identitas (e-KYC) ke Dukcapil dan menerbitkan 15 karakter SID di KSEI S-INVEST, yang berfungsi sebagai identitas unik pemodal di seluruh instrumen efek nasional.”

Who's Who & Accounts #ifua

IFUA (Investor Fund Unit Account)

🟢 In Plain Words (What It Really Is)

The sub-account under an investor's SID where their actual mutual fund units live. One investor has 1 SID, but multiple IFUAs (one per Selling Agent + Fund Manager/Custodian, or one per in-app Goal bucket).

🔵 System Analogy (How It Maps to What You Built)

If SID is your bank customer ID (CIF), IFUAs are your separate savings sub-accounts/pockets for Emergency Fund, Wedding, and Retirement.

🔴 How to Say It in the Interview

“We provision separate sub-IFUAs per portfolio goal in S-INVEST so FIFO unit cost basis stays isolated per goal and matches KSEI AKSes.”

🇮🇩 Definisi & Kalimat Wawancara (Bahasa Indonesia)

Pengertian: Nomor sub-rekening efek pemodal di KSEI untuk setiap produk reksa dana atau tujuan investasi.

Jawaban Wawancara: “Untuk mendukung fitur multi-tujuan investasi (goal-based investing) di aplikasi, arsitektur kami membuka sub-rekening IFUA terpisah di KSEI untuk setiap tujuan pemodal, sehingga perhitungan biaya perolehan (FIFO cost basis) di laporan KSEI AKSes tetap selaras 1-ke-1 dengan tampilan UI aplikasi.”

Who's Who & Accounts #rdn-escrow

RDN vs. Rekening Tampungan Reksa Dana

🟢 In Plain Words (What It Really Is)

RDN is a personal cash wallet bank account opened in the investor's own name. Rekening Tampungan (Escrow / Fund Collection Account) is a segregated pass-through account used to collect Virtual Account payments and sweep them to the Custodian Bank.

🔵 System Analogy (How It Maps to What You Built)

RDN is your personal wallet balance; Rekening Tampungan is a checkout transit pipe that must be emptied into the Custodian Bank before 13:00 WIB.

🔴 How to Say It in the Interview

“Whether we collect via RDN auto-debit or Closed Virtual Accounts into a segregated collection account, anti-commingling rules forbid mixing investor cash with company operating funds.”

Rules, Fees & Tax #kik

Reksa Dana KIK (Kontrak Investasi Kolektif)

🟢 In Plain Words (What It Really Is)

The legal structure of Indonesian mutual funds: a 3-party contract signed between the Fund Manager (MI) and Custodian Bank (BK) where investors buy fractional units. The fund's assets are legally separate from all companies' balance sheets.

🔵 System Analogy (How It Maps to What You Built)

Like a ring-fenced trust account: even if the app or Fund Manager goes bankrupt, the investor's assets stay safe at the Custodian Bank.

🔴 How to Say It in the Interview

“Under POJK 23/2016, Reksa Dana KIK assets are legally segregated at the Bank Kustodian and never enter the APERD's balance sheet.”

Buying, Selling & Pricing #nab-up

NAB / UP (Nilai Aktiva Bersih per Unit Penyertaan / NAV)

🟢 In Plain Words (What It Really Is)

The price of 1 mutual fund unit on a given day (calculated as Total Fund Assets minus Daily Fees/Liabilities, divided by Total Units in circulation). Units are tracked to 4 decimal places (e.g., 800.1234 UP).

🔵 System Analogy (How It Maps to What You Built)

Like the daily exchange rate of a currency or gram price of gold, except it is only calculated once a day after the market closes at 16:00 WIB.

🔴 How to Say It in the Interview

“Because NAB/UP is computed to 4 decimal places after market close, our schema stores unit quantities and prices as exact fixed-point decimals, never floating point.”

🇮🇩 Definisi & Kalimat Wawancara (Bahasa Indonesia)

Pengertian: Harga per 1 lembar unit penyertaan reksa dana (dihitung hingga 4 angka desimal).

Jawaban Wawancara: “Dalam reksa dana berlaku forward pricing: pemodal memasukkan nominal rupiah pada jam 10:00 WIB, tetapi harga beli per unit (NAB/UP) baru ditetapkan pada sore hari setelah penutupan pasar saham pukul 16:00 WIB.”

Buying, Selling & Pricing #forward-pricing

Forward Pricing (Blind / Unknown NAV at Checkout)

🟢 In Plain Words (What It Really Is)

When you buy or sell a mutual fund at 10:00 AM, nobody knows today's unit price yet! The app shows yesterday's price (NAB T-1), but your order actually executes at today's closing price (NAB T), published tomorrow morning (T+1).

🔵 System Analogy (How It Maps to What You Built)

Unlike e-commerce where price is fixed at checkout, mutual funds work like pre-ordering at today's closing market rate.

🔴 How to Say It in the Interview

“Forward pricing is why subscriptions are entered in nominal Rupiah while redemptions are executed in units, and why partial nominal redemptions need a volatility buffer.”

🇮🇩 Definisi & Kalimat Wawancara (Bahasa Indonesia)

Pengertian: Aturan di mana harga beli atau jual ditentukan pada penutupan bursa hari yang sama, bukan saat klik beli.

Jawaban Wawancara: “Forward pricing adalah pembeda utama sistem reksa dana dibandingkan e-commerce atau ticketing. Pada saat transaksi dibuat, harga final belum ada, sehingga sistem harus menyimpan status pending settlement hingga NAB resmi diterbitkan oleh Bank Kustodian pada pagi hari T+1.”

Buying, Selling & Pricing #cutoff

Cut-Off Time (13:00 WIB Hari Bursa)

🟢 In Plain Words (What It Really Is)

The daily 1:00 PM Jakarta time deadline on stock exchange business days. If your order AND your money arrive before 13:00 WIB, you get today's price (NAB T). If either is 1 second late (13:00:01), you get tomorrow's price (NAB T+1).

🔵 System Analogy (How It Maps to What You Built)

Like the check-in gate closing for a flight: miss 13:00 WIB and you are automatically moved to tomorrow's flight.

🔴 How to Say It in the Interview

“We enforce an internal 12:30 WIB payment buffer on aggregator Virtual Accounts so interbank sweeps land at the Bank Kustodian before the 13:00 WIB cut-off.”

🇮🇩 Definisi & Kalimat Wawancara (Bahasa Indonesia)

Pengertian: Batas waktu jam 13:00 WIB setiap Hari Bursa untuk mendapatkan harga NAB penutupan hari ini.

Jawaban Wawancara: “Aturan batas waktu 13:00 WIB bersifat dua pintu (dual-clock): baik instruksi pesanan di S-INVEST maupun dana efektif di rekening Bank Kustodian wajib tiba sebelum pukul 13:00 WIB. Keterlambatan salah satu pintu akan menggulirkan harga transaksi ke NAB hari bursa berikutnya (T+1).”

Buying, Selling & Pricing #hari-bursa

Hari Bursa (Exchange Business Day)

🟢 In Plain Words (What It Really Is)

A day when the Indonesia Stock Exchange (IDX / BEI) is open for trading (Monday-Friday, excluding national holidays and joint leave days). Mutual fund clocks (T+0, T+1, T+7) only count Hari Bursa, never weekends.

🔵 System Analogy (How It Maps to What You Built)

Like banking working days: an order placed on Saturday at 10:00 AM is treated as a Monday morning order.

🔴 How to Say It in the Interview

“Our cut-off bucketing engine must join against an authoritative IDX Hari Bursa holiday calendar table, never simple weekday math.”

Buying, Selling & Pricing #good-order

Good Order

🟢 In Plain Words (What It Really Is)

A valid, complete transaction instruction (active SID/IFUA, suitability passed, above minimum amount) received and registered in KSEI S-INVEST before 13:00 WIB.

🔵 System Analogy (How It Maps to What You Built)

Half 1 of the ticket: the booking form is valid and registered in the central system.

🔴 How to Say It in the Interview

“Good Order alone does not lock today's NAV; it must be matched with Good Funds at the Bank Kustodian before 13:00 WIB.”

🇮🇩 Definisi & Kalimat Wawancara (Bahasa Indonesia)

Pengertian: Status instruksi pesanan yang lengkap, lolos validasi profil risiko, dan terdaftar di S-INVEST.

Jawaban Wawancara: “Status Good Order memastikan bahwa seluruh parameter pesanan, verifikasi SID/IFUA, dan konfirmasi profil risiko telah tervalidasi secara sistemik sebelum instruksi dikirim ke KSEI.”

Payments & Reconciliation #good-funds

Good Funds (Dana Efektif)

🟢 In Plain Words (What It Really Is)

Cash that has actually arrived, cleared, and been matched inside the Mutual Fund's account at the Bank Kustodian (BK) before 13:00 WIB - NOT merely a 'paid' webhook from a payment gateway.

🔵 System Analogy (How It Maps to What You Built)

Half 2 of the ticket: the money is physically in the Custodian Bank's vault, not stuck in transit at a payment gateway.

🔴 How to Say It in the Interview

“A payment gateway 'settlement' callback is only an advisory hint; Good Funds is determined exclusively by the Bank Kustodian's account credit timestamp.”

🇮🇩 Definisi & Kalimat Wawancara (Bahasa Indonesia)

Pengertian: Status dana pembayaran pemodal yang telah efektif masuk ke rekening Bank Kustodian sebelum batas 13:00 WIB.

Jawaban Wawancara: “Webhook sukses dari payment gateway saja tidak cukup untuk menandai Good Funds; sistem kami wajib memastikan sweep dana antar-bank telah efektif mengendap di rekening reksa dana Bank Kustodian sebelum batas cut-off 13:00 WIB.”

Buying, Selling & Pricing #subscription

Subscription (Pembelian Unit)

🟢 In Plain Words (What It Really Is)

Buying a mutual fund. The investor specifies a Rupiah amount (e.g., Rp 1,000,000), pays via VA/RDN/QRIS, and receives 4-decimal units (UP) on T+1 morning after the closing price (NAB T) is published.

🔵 System Analogy (How It Maps to What You Built)

Topping up Rupiah to convert into fractional fund units once today's closing rate is known.

🔴 How to Say It in the Interview

“On a Subscription, our OMS tracks two asynchronous phases: cash settlement (Good Funds) on Day T and S-INVEST unit allotment on Day T+1.”

🇮🇩 Definisi & Kalimat Wawancara (Bahasa Indonesia)

Pengertian: Transaksi pembelian unit penyertaan reksa dana oleh pemodal.

Jawaban Wawancara: “Alur subscription memproses konversi dari nominal Rupiah kotor menjadi lembar unit penyertaan bersih setelah dikurangi biaya pembelian (subscription fee) dan dibagi dengan harga NAB penutupan.”

Buying, Selling & Pricing #redemption

Redemption (Penjualan Kembali / Pencairan)

🟢 In Plain Words (What It Really Is)

Selling mutual fund units back into cash. The investor specifies how many Units (UP) to sell; units are burned at closing price (NAB T), and the Bank Kustodian wires cash directly to the investor's bank within T+1 to max T+7 Exchange Days.

🔵 System Analogy (How It Maps to What You Built)

Cashing out units: the Custodian Bank wires the proceeds directly to the user's registered bank account.

🔴 How to Say It in the Interview

“During Redemption, APERD never touches the payout cash; the Bank Kustodian wires funds directly to the investor's S-INVEST registered bank account within the statutory T+7 cap.”

🇮🇩 Definisi & Kalimat Wawancara (Bahasa Indonesia)

Pengertian: Transaksi penjualan kembali unit penyertaan reksa dana untuk dicairkan menjadi uang tunai.

Jawaban Wawancara: “Dalam transaksi redemption, pemodal menjual lembar unit penyertaan, dan dana hasil penjualan ditransfer langsung oleh Bank Kustodian ke rekening bank pemodal yang terdaftar di KSEI (paling lambat T+7 hari bursa).”

Buying, Selling & Pricing #switching

Switching (Pengalihan Unit)

🟢 In Plain Words (What It Really Is)

Moving money directly from Fund A to Fund B. Native S-INVEST Switching only works between two funds managed by the SAME Manajer Investasi (MI). Switching across different MIs requires selling Fund A first, waiting T+2 for cash in RDN, then buying Fund B.

🔵 System Analogy (How It Maps to What You Built)

Same-MI Switch is a direct connecting flight on one airline; Cross-MI Switch is landing, collecting baggage (waiting T+2 for cash), and re-booking on another airline.

🔴 How to Say It in the Interview

“Our BRD distinguishes native 1-step S-INVEST switching within the same MI from synthetic 2-leg cross-MI switching via RDN, which carries a 2-day NAV gap.”

🇮🇩 Definisi & Kalimat Wawancara (Bahasa Indonesia)

Pengertian: Transaksi pengalihan investasi dari Reksa Dana A ke Reksa Dana B.

Jawaban Wawancara: “Pengalihan dalam Manajer Investasi yang sama (native switching) diproses secara atomik di S-INVEST pada hari yang sama (T+0). Sedangkan pengalihan antar-MI memerlukan orkestrasi sintetis 2 tahap (redemption lalu subscription ulang) dengan jeda waktu penyelesaian dana sekitar 2 hari bursa.”

Buying, Selling & Pricing #min-balance

Saldo Minimum Kepemilikan (Minimum Remaining Balance)

🟢 In Plain Words (What It Really Is)

The minimum unit/Rupiah value an investor must keep in a fund after a partial sale (e.g., Rp 100,000). If a market drop at 16:00 WIB pushes their remaining balance below this floor, the partial sale is either rejected or forced into 'Sell All Units'.

🔵 System Analogy (How It Maps to What You Built)

Like a bank account minimum balance rule, complicated by the fact that the afternoon market price can move by 1-3%.

🔴 How to Say It in the Interview

“To prevent partial nominal redemptions from failing EOD validation at S-INVEST, we apply a 10% volatility buffer or prompt the user to Redeem All Units.”

Rules, Fees & Tax #trailer-fee

Trailer Fee (Management Fee Sharing)

🟢 In Plain Words (What It Really Is)

How zero-fee fintech APERD apps make recurring revenue: the Fund Manager (MI) shares a slice of its annual Management Fee (e.g., 0.3%-0.8% per year of the total assets held by your app's users), calculated daily and paid monthly.

🔵 System Analogy (How It Maps to What You Built)

Like an ongoing affiliate revenue share on the total balance your users keep invested.

🔴 How to Say It in the Interview

“Even when front-end subscription fees are 0%, accurate daily unitholder balance reconciliation is critical because our monthly Trailer Fee accrual from each MI depends on daily settled AUM.”

Rules, Fees & Tax #tax

Bukan Objek Pajak (0% Mutual Fund Income Tax)

🟢 In Plain Words (What It Really Is)

In Indonesia (UU PPh Pasal 4 ayat 3 huruf i), mutual fund profits and dividends are NOT taxed when the investor sells or receives payouts (0% tax deduction), because taxes were already paid inside the fund portfolio. Investors only report the balance as an Asset (Harta Kode 036) on their annual SPT.

🔵 System Analogy (How It Maps to What You Built)

Tax-free take-home payout for the user - no withholding tax calculation needed on redemption.

🔴 How to Say It in the Interview

“Mutual fund capital gains and PHI distributions are Bukan Objek Pajak to the unitholder, though we provide annual e-statements for their SPT Harta reporting.”

🇮🇩 Definisi & Kalimat Wawancara (Bahasa Indonesia)

Pengertian: Keuntungan investasi reksa dana (capital gain) bukan merupakan objek pajak penghasilan di Indonesia.

Jawaban Wawancara: “Berdasarkan UU PPh Pasal 4 Ayat 3 huruf i, imbal hasil reksa dana bukan merupakan objek PPh, sehingga sistem kami tidak melakukan pemotongan pajak saat pemodal melakukan redemption.”

Buying, Selling & Pricing #phi

PHI (Pembagian Hasil Investasi / Dividend)

🟢 In Plain Words (What It Really Is)

Periodic profit distributions paid by some Fixed Income funds, either wired as Cash by the Custodian Bank or automatically reinvested into new Units (UP) in S-INVEST without the user placing an order.

🔵 System Analogy (How It Maps to What You Built)

Like automatic interest/dividend reinvestment that adds units to your account overnight.

🔴 How to Say It in the Interview

“Our daily S-INVEST ingestion pipeline must process PHI dividend unit reinvestment files; otherwise, our local unit balance will drift below KSEI (Exception XU-12).”

🇮🇩 Definisi & Kalimat Wawancara (Bahasa Indonesia)

Pengertian: Pembagian Hasil Investasi (dividen) dari reksa dana pendapatan tetap dalam bentuk uang tunai atau reinvestasi unit.

Jawaban Wawancara: “File aksi korporasi PHI dari KSEI S-INVEST wajib diproses secara otomatis oleh mesin harian agar saldo lembar unit pemodal tidak mengalami selisih (break XU-12).”

Rules, Fees & Tax #suitability

Profil Risiko & Suitability Mismatch Gate (SEOJK 51/2016)

🟢 In Plain Words (What It Really Is)

Every investor takes a quiz to get a Risk Profile (1=Conservative to 5=Aggressive), and every fund has a Risk Score (1 to 5). If a user buys a fund riskier than their profile, the app MUST show a warning and log their explicit consent.

🔵 System Analogy (How It Maps to What You Built)

Like an age/safety waiver before buying a high-risk product, stored in an immutable audit log for OJK inspectors.

🔴 How to Say It in the Interview

“Per SEOJK 51/2016, every order snapshots the investor's risk profile score and logs an immutable mismatch consent if Fund Risk exceeds Investor Risk.”

🇮🇩 Definisi & Kalimat Wawancara (Bahasa Indonesia)

Pengertian: Kewajiban regulasi OJK untuk mencocokkan profil risiko pemodal dengan tingkat risiko produk reksa dana.

Jawaban Wawancara: “Sesuai SEOJK 51/2016, jika pemodal dengan profil konservatif memilih reksa dana saham berisiko tinggi, sistem kami wajib menampilkan peringatan kesesuaian dan mencatat bukti persetujuan risiko (risk mismatch consent) yang dapat diaudit oleh OJK.”

Rules, Fees & Tax #apu-ppt

APU-PPT, e-KYC & Third-Party Payer Rejection

🟢 In Plain Words (What It Really Is)

Anti-Money Laundering rules (POJK 12/2017 & POJK 8/2023): identity is verified via KTP + selfie liveness + Dukcapil, screened against PEP/terrorist lists, and payments/redemptions MUST come from/go to a bank account in the exact same legal name as the investor's SID.

🔵 System Analogy (How It Maps to What You Built)

Strict name-matching security: you cannot pay for your mutual fund using your spouse's or friend's bank account.

🔴 How to Say It in the Interview

“If funds arrive from a third-party bank account name that doesn't match the SID, the Bank Kustodian flags an APU-PPT mismatch and parks the cash in suspense for manual refund.”

Payments & Reconciliation #attempt-idempotency

Invoice vs. Attempt (-a1 / -a2) & 4 Idempotency Keys

🟢 In Plain Words (What It Really Is)

Separating the parent Order/Invoice (`INV-001`) from each 15-minute payment attempt (`INV-001-a1`, `INV-001-a2`) so an expired QRIS/VA can be retried cleanly, guarded by 4 deduplication keys (`K_charge`, `K_delivery`, `K_settle`, `K_allot`) and monotonic status ranks.

🔵 System Analogy (How It Maps to What You Built)

The exact architecture you designed in your payment staging plan: retrying a payment ticket never creates a duplicate order or double unit allotment.

🔴 How to Say It in the Interview

“We decouple invoice state from attempt state (`-a1`, `-a2`) and enforce raw SHA-512 verification plus a mandatory server-to-server GET /status back-check.”

Payments & Reconciliation #recon-3way

3-Way Daily Reconciliation (OMS vs. BK Cash vs. S-INVEST Units)

🟢 In Plain Words (What It Really Is)

The daily automated audit that compares three separate lists: (1) Orders in your App DB, (2) Real Money in the Custodian Bank Statement, and (3) Real Units allotted in KSEI S-INVEST - catching breaks like 'Paid but No Units' (`XU-01`) or 'Units without Cash' (`XU-02`).

🔵 System Analogy (How It Maps to What You Built)

Like matching your store receipts, your bank statement, and the warehouse inventory shipment log every afternoon.

🔴 How to Say It in the Interview

“Because cash settles at the Bank Kustodian while units settle in KSEI S-INVEST on T+1, a 3-way reconciliation engine is mandatory to catch and resolve S1 breaks within SLA.”

Blueprint 01 // End-to-End Subscription (Buy Flow) [ Follow Steps 01 → 08 · Left-to-Right Reading Order ]

What Happens When an Investor Buys Rp 1,000,000 of a Mutual Fund

Instead of memorizing 7 separate companies at once, follow the numbered steps from ▶ START (Step 01) at Top-Left to ✓ END (Step 08) at Bottom-Right. Row 1 happens before the 13:00 WIB Cut-Off on Day T; Row 2 happens from 16:00 WIB Market Close through Day T+1 Unit Allotment.

Pyramid Communication · The Bottom-Line Answer (BLUF)

When an investor buys Rp 1,000,000 of a mutual fund, the APERD app acts solely as an order router: the money moves directly to the segregated Bank Kustodian account, the order instruction routes to KSEI S-INVEST, and 800.0000 units are allotted the next morning at the official closing price (NAB_T) with Rp 0 cash float held by the app.

Pillar 01 · Money Safety
Segregated Custody

Investor cash lands in the Fund Account at the Bank Kustodian, legally separate from APERD balance sheet.

Pillar 02 · Dual Cut-Off
13:00 WIB Boundary

Both S-INVEST Good Order and Bank Good Funds must arrive ≤ 13:00 WIB to lock today's price (NAB_T).

Pillar 03 · Unit Authority
KSEI S-INVEST Allotment

KSEI computes units to 4 decimal places on T+1; validated via 3-way daily automated reconciliation.

Mathematical Formulation 03: FIFO Redemption Fee & Net Payout POJK 23/2016 Compliant
$$Gross_{proceeds} = UP_{redeemed} \times NAB_t$$ $$Fee_{redemption} = \sum_{i=1}^{k} \left( UP_i \times NAB_t \times r(HoldingDays_i) \right)$$ $$Net_{disbursement} = Gross_{proceeds} - Fee_{redemption} - Fee_{bi\_fast}$$

FIFO Accounting Law: Units are liquidated in the exact chronological order of their allotment dates. If tranche $i$ was held for $< 365$ days, fee rate $r$ applies; if held $\ge 365$ days, $r = 0\%$. Under UU PPh Pasal 4 Ayat 3(i), tax withholding is $0\%$ (Bukan Objek Pajak).

🆔 Why 1 Investor Has Multiple IFUAs (Goal Buckets)

If a user creates 3 Goals in your app (Emergency Fund, Wedding, Retirement) and buys the same Money Market Fund in all 3, pooling them under 1 IFUA merges their FIFO Unit Cost Basis at KSEI. Opening separate Sub-IFUAs per Goal keeps KSEI AKSes Statements 1-to-1 with your app UI.

🏦 RDN vs. Rekening Tampungan Reksa Dana (Escrow)

RDN (Rekening Dana Nasabah) is a bank account opened in the investor's own name & NIK (common in securities-company APERDs). Rekening Tampungan / Fund Collection Account is a segregated custody/escrow account where Virtual Account payments sweep before 13:00 WIB.

Order is registered in KSEI S-INVEST AND cash is credited inside the Bank Kustodian Account before 13:00 WIB. Receives today's closing price (NAB Day T, published T+1 Morning).

T+1 NAV · Post-Cut-Off Order

3. Order Placed After 13:00 WIB (or on Weekend)

Any order submitted at 13:00:01 WIB on Tuesday rolls to Wednesday's closing price (NAB T+1). Saturday/Sunday orders price at Monday's Closing NAV.

REJECTED · APU-PPT Suspense

4. Third-Party Bank Transfer (Name Mismatch)

Investor transfers from a spouse's or company bank account. Bank Kustodian rejects funds under POJK 8/2023 (APU-PPT) anti-money-laundering rules and holds cash in suspense (XC-02) for manual refund.

Mathematical Formulation 01: Forward Pricing & 4-Decimal Unit Allotment KSEI S-INVEST Standard
$$UP_{allotted} = \left\lfloor \frac{Nominal_{net}}{NAB_t} \right\rfloor_4 = \left\lfloor \frac{Rp\,1{,}000{,}000}{Rp\,1{,}250.0000} \right\rfloor_4 = 800.0000\text{ UP}$$

Governing Rule: All mutual fund units are truncated (floor) to exactly 4 decimal places per KSEI S-INVEST specification, avoiding fractional accumulation drift.

Formula A: Deduct Fee from Gross Method A (Multiplicative)
$$Nominal_{net} = Nominal_{gross} \times (1 - Fee_{sub})$$ $$UP = \left\lfloor \frac{Nominal_{gross} \times (1 - Fee_{sub})}{NAB_t} \right\rfloor_4$$

Example: $Rp\,1{,}000{,}000 \times (1 - 0.01) = Rp\,990{,}000$ → $\frac{990{,}000}{1{,}250.0000} = \mathbf{792.0000\text{ UP}}$.

Formula B: Gross Divided by (1 + Fee) Method B (Divisive)
$$Nominal_{net} = \frac{Nominal_{gross}}{1 + Fee_{sub}}$$ $$UP = \left\lfloor \frac{Nominal_{gross}}{(1 + Fee_{sub}) \times NAB_t} \right\rfloor_4$$

Example: $\frac{Rp\,1{,}000{,}000}{1.01} = Rp\,990{,}099.01$ → $\frac{990{,}099.01}{1{,}250.0000} = \mathbf{792.0792\text{ UP}}$.

Mathematical Formulation 02: Partial Redemption Volatility Buffer Risk Mitigation
$$\left( UP_{remaining} \times NAB_{t-1} \times (1 - \sigma_{buffer}) \right) \ge Balance_{minimum}$$

BRD Safety Condition: Because today's closing $NAB_t$ is unknown when a user places a partial Rupiah sell order at 11:00 WIB, the checkout engine applies a safety factor $\sigma_{buffer} \ge 0.10$ (10% market cushion) to prevent the remaining balance from breaching the fund's mandatory minimum holding threshold (Saldo Minimum Kepemilikan).

🧮 Subscription Fee & 4-Decimal Unit Formulas

Different Manajer Investasi (MI) Prospectuses define front-end subscription fees using one of two formulas (your Product Master table needs a fee_calc_mode column):

Method A (Deduct Fee from Gross):
Net = Gross × (1 - Fee%) = Rp 1,000,000 × 0.99 = Rp 990,000
Units = 990,000 ÷ 1,250.0000 = 792.0000 UP

Method B (Divide Gross by 1 + Fee%):
Net = Gross ÷ (1 + Fee%) = Rp 1,000,000 ÷ 1.01 = Rp 990,099.01
Units = 990,099.01 ÷ 1,250.0000 = 792.0792 UP

⚠️ The Partial Redemption "Minimum Balance" Trap

Because NAB Day T is unknown when a user sells, a partial Rupiah redemption can drop their remaining balance below the fund's Minimum Holding Threshold if the market falls at 16:00 WIB:

Holdings @ Yesterday's NAV: Rp 1,050,000 (Min Hold: Rp 100,000)
User Redeems: Rp 940,000 (Leaves Rp 110,000 estimated)
16:00 WIB Market Drops 2% → Remaining Units Worth Rp 91,000 (< 100k!)
BRD Fix: Enforce a 10% Volatility Buffer or auto-convert to Redeem All Units.

Section 07 // Domain Gap Analysis [ How Mutual Funds Differ from Fixed-Price E-Commerce ]

8 Mutual Fund Mechanics That Don't Exist in E-Commerce or Ticketing

GAP 01🧮

Forward Pricing (Unknown NAV)

You buy in Nominal Rupiah (Rp), but sell/switch in 4-Decimal Units (UP) priced at today's 16:00 WIB close.

Trap: Partial Rp redemptions can breach Min Remaining Balance if NAV drops → force Redeem All Units buffer.
GAP 02⏱️

Dual-Clock 13:00 WIB Cut-Off

Gateway webhook ≠ Good Funds. Cash must settle inside the Bank Kustodian Account before 13:00 WIB.

Trap: Aggregator VAs need a 12:30 WIB internal buffer so interbank sweeps land before 13:00 WIB.
GAP 03🏛️

KSEI S-INVEST & Multi-IFUA

1 Investor holds 1 Master SID (15 chars) but separate IFUA Sub-Accounts per MI/BK and per in-app Goal.

Trap: Pooling multiple app goals in 1 IFUA scrambles FIFO Cost Basis on KSEI AKSes statements.
GAP 04🔀

Native vs. Cross-MI Switching

KSEI S-INVEST only supports native atomic switching within the Same Manajer Investasi (MI).

Trap: Cross-MI switch requires a 2-Leg Chain (Redeem → RDN at T+2 → Buy) with 2-day NAV slippage.
GAP 05💸

Direct BK Redemption Payout

Bank Kustodian wires redemption proceeds directly to the investor's bank (Max T+7) and deducts transfer fees.

Trap: Bank account changes must sync to KSEI S-INVEST before redeeming or BK wires to the old account.
GAP 06📊

0% Tax, Trailer Fees & FIFO

Mutual fund gains are Bukan Objek Pajak (0% Tax). Management fees accrue daily inside NAV; APERD earns Trailer Fee.

Trap: Must support both Multiply and Divide subscription fee formulas per Fund Prospectus.
GAP 07🎁

Dividend (PHI) Reinvestment

Fixed-income funds distribute dividends (PHI) as cash or auto-reinvested units posted directly in S-INVEST.

Trap: Ignoring S-INVEST PHI dividend files causes XU-12 unitholder balance drift.
GAP 08🛡️

SEOJK 51/2016 Suitability Gate

If a Conservative investor (Level 1) buys an Equity Fund (Level 5), checkout must log an immutable Mismatch Consent.

Trap: Electronic Prospectus Ack + Risk Mismatch Log are mandatory OJK Audit Artifacts.
Section 08 // Interview Self-Test [ Click Each Question to Reveal the Architecture Answer ]

5 Interview Trap Questions (Click to Test Yourself)

1. "An investor pays a Virtual Account at 12:56 WIB and our gateway webhook fires at 12:57 WIB. Are they guaranteed to get today's NAV?"+
No. Under POJK 23/2016, receiving Day T NAV (NAB_T) requires BOTH the validated order in S-INVEST (Good Order) AND settled cash inside the Bank Kustodian's Fund Account (Good Funds) before 13:00 WIB. If the gateway collects into an aggregator escrow that sweeps to the Custodian Bank at 13:05 WIB, the Custodian stamps it post-cut-off and rolls pricing to NAB Day T+1.
2. "Why can a partial Rupiah redemption fail at 17:00 WIB even though the user left enough minimum balance at 11:00 WIB?"+
Forward Pricing Volatility. At 11:00 WIB the app only knows yesterday's NAV (NAB T-1). If the market drops 2% at 16:00 WIB close, the remaining units at NAB_T may fall below the fund's Saldo Minimum Kepemilikan. The BRD must enforce a volatility buffer (e.g., 10% above minimum balance) or auto-convert the instruction to Redeem All Units.
3. "Can a user switch directly from Mandiri Investa Pasar Uang to Schroder Dana Prestasi Plus in one S-INVEST Switching order?"+
No. Native KSEI S-INVEST Switching only works within the Same Manajer Investasi (MI) (and eligible Custodian per Prospectus). Cross-MI switching requires orchestrating a 2-Leg Synthetic Workflow: Leg 1 redeems Fund A (T+0) into the investor's RDN (settling T+2), and Leg 2 auto-subscribes Fund B once RDN cash clears - which exposes the investor to a 2-Day NAV Gap.
4. "An investor updates their payout bank account in our app and immediately clicks Redeem. What happens if we send the redemption right away?"+
The Bank Kustodian will wire the money to their old bank account (or bounce). APERD does not disburse redemption cash - the Bank Kustodian wires proceeds to the bank account recorded in KSEI S-INVEST. Checkout must gate the redemption until the S-INVEST Investor Bank Amendment callback confirms the new account is active.
5. "What happens if a payment gateway sends 3 duplicate webhooks, or a 'pending' webhook arrives after 'settlement'?"+
Our webhook handler enforces a 4-Gate Checkpoint: (1) raw-byte SHA-512 Signature verification, (2) mandatory server-to-server GET /status poll, (3) 4-grain idempotency keys (K_charge, K_delivery, K_settle, K_allot), and (4) Monotonic State Rank Law (SETTLED=50 > PENDING=20) so out-of-order regressions and replays become harmless 200 OK no-ops.

5 Jawaban Pertanyaan Wawancara Teknis dalam Bahasa Indonesia

1. “Jika nasabah membayar VA pada 12:56 WIB dan webhook payment gateway sukses pada 12:57 WIB, apakah dijamin mendapat NAB hari ini?”+
Jawaban Tegas: Belum Tentu. Sesuai POJK 23/2016, pemodal berhak atas NAB hari ini (NAB_T) hanya jika terpenuhi dua kondisi sekaligus sebelum pukul 13:00 WIB: (1) Good Order di S-INVEST dan (2) Good Funds di rekening Bank Kustodian. Jika payment gateway mengumpulkan dana ke rekening penampung aggregator dan baru menyetorkan kas ke Bank Kustodian pada pukul 13:05 WIB, Bank Kustodian akan mencap dana tersebut sebagai late funds, sehingga transaksi otomatis bergulir ke harga NAB T+1. Solusi arsitektur kami adalah menetapkan batas internal pembayaran VA pada 12:30 WIB.
2. “Mengapa penjualan sebagian nominal Rupiah (partial redemption) bisa gagal pada sore hari padahal paginya sisa saldo nasabah masih cukup?”+
Jawaban: Akibat Volatilitas Forward Pricing. Pada jam 10:00 WIB, aplikasi hanya mengetahui NAB kemarin (NAB T-1). Jika pasar saham anjlok 2% pada penutupan bursa pukul 16:00 WIB, nilai unit riil yang tersisa saat penetapan NAB_T dapat jatuh di bawah Saldo Minimum Kepemilikan yang ditetapkan dalam Prospektus. Solusi arsitektur kami adalah menerapkan buffer volatilitas minimal 10% di atas saldo minimum, atau secara otomatis mengubah pesanan menjadi Jual Seluruh Unit (Redeem All Units).
3. “Apakah nasabah bisa melakukan switching langsung dari Reksa Dana Pasar Uang Mandiri ke Reksa Dana Saham Schroder dalam satu instruksi S-INVEST?”+
Jawaban: Tidak Bisa Secara Native. Fitur switching atomik di KSEI S-INVEST hanya didukung untuk produk di bawah Manajer Investasi (MI) yang sama. Untuk pengalihan lintas MI (Mandiri ke Schroder), arsitektur kami wajib mengorkestrasi alur sintetis 2 tahap: Tahap 1 melakukan redemption Reksa Dana A ke RDN nasabah (selesai T+2), dan Tahap 2 secara otomatis mengeksekusi subscription ke Reksa Dana B saat dana RDN telah efektif. Hal ini menimbulkan jeda NAB 2 hari kerja yang wajib disetujui pemodal di awal.
4. “Nasabah mengubah nomor rekening bank pencairan di aplikasi kami dan langsung menekan tombol Redeem. Apa yang terjadi jika pesanan langsung dikirim?”+
Jawaban: Dana akan ditransfer ke rekening lama nasabah atau gagal (bouncing). APERD tidak mentransfer uang pencairan; Bank Kustodian yang mentransfer dana langsung ke rekening bank yang tercatat resmi di KSEI S-INVEST. Sistem kami wajib menahan (gate) pesanan redemption hingga proses Investor Bank Amendment di S-INVEST mendapatkan notifikasi approval dari KSEI.
5. “Bagaimana arsitektur Anda menangani duplikasi webhook dari payment gateway atau webhook 'pending' yang datang setelah 'settlement'?”+
Jawaban: Melalui 4 Lapisan Pengamanan Webhook. (1) Validasi signature SHA-512 dari raw-bytes, (2) Pengecekan status balik (server-to-server GET /status) ke endpoint payment gateway, (3) 4 kunci idempoten granular (K_charge, K_delivery, K_settle, K_allot), dan (4) Aturan Monotonik Status State-Machine di mana level SETTLED (50) > PENDING (20), sehingga pesan out-of-order atau replay 3 kali secara aman diabaikan (safe no-op dengan HTTP 200 OK).
