🎨 Physical Canvas Discipline

Stepping Outside the Screen: No Cmd+Z on Belgian Linen

In software, rollback is cheap. On physical linen with heavy oil paint, every stroke of the palette knife is permanent. Once paint oxidizes, you either integrate the texture or ruin the canvas.

That physical finality is why I build systems with obsessive defensive margin: respecting real-world friction, material constraints, and the irrecoverable cost of failure.
