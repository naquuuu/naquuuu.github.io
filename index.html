Hey, I'm Krishna (naquuuu). Most tech today assumes high-speed WiFi, air-conditioned server rooms, and infinite cloud power. But Indonesia is an archipelago of 17,000 islands where internet drops on open water, hardware takes a beating in tropical humidity, and systems must work anyway.

At 18, my first real job was answering 10,000 panicking high school students whose university entrance exam prep crashed late at night. That taught me one permanent rule: technology is meaningless if it doesn't solve real human anxiety.

With an Industrial Engineering foundation from Institut Teknologi Bandung (ITB), I turn messy operational workflows into automated, failure-proof software—from omnichannel retail loyalty at MAPCLUB to high-stress flight refund engines at Traveloka and industrial mega-energy projects at BCG.

Healthcare & Eco-Materials ★ 3M Regional Award

3M PHB Bio-Mask: 100% Biodegradable PPE

Billions of plastic medical masks pollute our oceans and take 450+ years to break down. We engineered a surgical mask made from bacterial biopolymers (PHB) grown on Indonesian sugarcane waste that safely composts in soil in 90 days.

Clean Energy Storage ★ Schneider National Winner

Schneider HotSand: Storing Solar Heat in Sand

Imported lithium batteries are expensive and degrade fast in humid tropical climates. HotSand stores excess daytime solar energy in superheated local silica sand beds to provide hot water, crop drying, and clean power overnight.
