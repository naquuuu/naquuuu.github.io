✦ switch theme & perspective:

murphy's law is my working philosophy: whatever can fail, will fail. so i sweat the details and map every scenario before it maps me. but scenarios don't only live in systems. art, culture, and politics each hold possibilities that engineering alone can't see.

that's why this portfolio runs two lenses. systems & matter holds the professional work: loyalty, refunds, energy models. culture & taste holds the rest: painting, playlists, tailoring. flip the switch and you get the other side of the same person.

partly inspired by my twin brother harya, i put this website together to share what i build and what i learn along the way. feel free to explore or reach out.

how i think about systems [ 04 principles ]
01 murphy's law
expect failure

plan for bad days

buses stall, signals drop, forms get messy. i assume that chaos in my designs, so people still get through calmly.

02 reka peluang
edge cases

map the edge cases early

"reka semua peluang yang ada." i list timeouts, race conditions, and wrong taps before i build, then test the ugly paths first.

03 buffers
margin

keep a little slack

i add retries, pauses, and extra space. that slack keeps systems steady when traffic jumps.

04 recovery
resilience

help people recover fast

when things fail, show clear status, save progress, and offer a next step. never leave a user with a blank screen or an unconfirmed transaction.
