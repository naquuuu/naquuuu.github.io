how i think about systems [ 04 simple principles ]
01 murphy's law
expect failure

expect things to break

delays, network drops, and messy inputs happen in the real world. i design systems assuming friction from day one instead of hoping everything works smoothly.

02 reka peluang
edge cases

map out the edge cases

"reka semua peluang yang ada." map out timeouts, race conditions, and user mistakes before writing code or building prototypes.

03 buffers
margin

build in buffers

give systems room to breathe. whether that's retry limits in flight APIs or thermal storage in hardware, good buffers absorb unexpected shocks.

04 recovery
resilience

recover cleanly

when errors happen, handle them smoothly. never leave a user with a blank screen or an unconfirmed transaction.

01 // physical canvas [ coffee shop in tebet ]

i painted this on a generic canvas with some paint at a coffee shop in tebet. no fancy belgian linen or dedicated studio setup—just spending an afternoon layering colors and seeing what happens.
