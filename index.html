The Radical Empathy Engine

empathy_loop.flow

From Answering 10,000 Panicking Students to Autonomous Squads

Right after high school at 18, my first real job wasn't a cozy office internship—it was customer support at EdukaSystem, answering the panic and confusion of over 10,000 students preparing for university entrance exams.

STEP 01
10,000 Panicking Students
High schoolers studying late at night when test servers crash. You learn that users don't care about coding buzzwords—they care whether your product solves their anxiety in seconds.
STEP 02
Listen for the Signal
Instead of treating complaints as annoying support tickets, every single bug report was treated as a treasure map showing where the product flow was confusing.
STEP 03
Break Functional Silos
At ITB, when leading the Professional Development division, I tore down rigid departmental walls and turned 20 students into fast, autonomous product squads with real ownership.
STEP 04
Ranked #1 with Real Trust
Organized hands-on offline immersions with tech leaders (Huawei, Tokopedia, Paragon) for 200+ students. Ranked #1 of 15 divisions in ITB with record team engagement.
💬 The Lesson for Any Young Builder: You don't need fancy jargon to make an impact. Listen deeply to real human frustration, find the root cause, and build a clean system that fixes it permanently.

Product Manager (Flight Refunds & Disruption Domain)
Traveloka • High-Scale Aviation Systems
2024 — 2026

When airlines cancel or delay flights, passengers are stressed and airlines have cryptic, confusing fare rules. We turned complex aviation regulation (PM 185/2015) into an automated, transparent system.

The Real-World Mess
Airline delays created massive customer anxiety and error-prone manual calculations between 10+ different flight APIs and tax rules.
The Engineering Fix
Overhauled calculation accuracy from 87.9% to 94.2% and automated cryptic airline ticket rules using smart parsing algorithms.
The Concrete Result
Doubled automated resolutions (16.8% → 34.4%), cut customer disputes in half, and unlocked ~Rp10 Billion in net business revenue in 3 months.

3M PHB Bio-Mask: 100% Biodegradable PPE

Billions of plastic surgical masks litter our oceans and take 450+ years to break down. We engineered a medical mask made from bacterial biopolymers (PHB) grown on Indonesian sugarcane waste that safely composts in soil within 90 days.

Clean Energy Storage ★ Schneider National Winner

Schneider HotSand: Storing Solar Heat in Sand

Imported lithium batteries are too expensive and break down rapidly in hot Indonesian humidity. HotSand stores excess daytime solar energy in superheated local silica sand beds to provide hot water, crop drying, and clean power at night.

The Physical Atelier: Canvas & Pigment

acrylic_on_linen.art

Why I Paint: Atoms Cannot Be Undone With a Git Commit

The textured dark red backdrop you see across this workstation is my original physical acrylic and oil painting on linen. In digital software, if you make a mistake, you can press Ctrl+Z or roll back a server. In physical painting, every sweep of the palette knife leaves real texture that cannot be erased.

This discipline carries straight into how I design physical products: measure twice, respect the natural materials, and craft with intention.
