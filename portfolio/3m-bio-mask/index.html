competition background & problem statement

During the 2021 3M Inspire Challenge, our student team, Team Kancil from Institut Teknologi Bandung (ITB), tackled the environmental surge of single-use personal protective equipment (PPE). Standard surgical masks rely on petroleum-derived polypropylene plastic that persists for centuries in landfills and waterways. Our proposal explored whether biodegradable biopolymers could form the basis for a more sustainable protective mask concept.

proposal overview [ concept & lifecycle ]
phb biopolymer material concept
The competition proposal explored utilizing Polyhydroxybutyrate (PHB) synthesized from cyanobacteria and reinforced with kenaf as a biodegradable, non-woven alternative to fossil-based surgical mask fibers.
3-ply structure & ergonomic fit concept
The submission modeled a 3-ply non-woven sheet (spunbond outer/inner plies and meltblown filter), with a roadmap feature using PHB's memory shape for improved ergonomic fit without conventional plastic waste.
awards & context [ competition outcomes ]
competition recognition
Presented at the 3M Inspire Challenge 2021, the proposal won 1st Place National Champion (Indonesia) and advanced to the Southeast Asia regional finals, securing 2nd Runner-Up across ASEAN participants.
academic concept note
This project was an academic competition proposal developed during Industrial Engineering studies at Institut Teknologi Bandung (ITB). It represents early conceptual exploration and techno-commercial structuring, not a tested clinical medical device or commercially manufactured product.
