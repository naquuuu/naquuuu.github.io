3M PHB Bio-Mask

1st place national: 3m asean • Polyhydroxybutyrate (PHB) • Industrial Engineering, ITB

the core idea

Single-use plastic masks create tons of waste that lasts for hundreds of years, while relying on fragile petroleum supply chains. We wanted to design medical-grade protection that keeps people safe during use, but breaks down naturally in soil afterwards.

degradation comparison [ 450 years vs 6-12 months ]
standard surgical mask // polypropylene plastic
450+ years

takes centuries to break down. fragments into persistent microplastics that accumulate in waterways and soil.

3m bio-mask // sugarcane phb biopolymer
6–12 months

breaks down naturally in ordinary soil and compost within 6 to 12 months into harmless water and co2, leaving zero microplastics.

facial seal & comfort [ 3d ergonomics ]
standard flat-pleated mask
30–40% edge leakage

flat-fold gaps around the nose and jawline let unfiltered air bypass the filter entirely during normal breathing.

3m 3d molded ergonomic seal
< 2% edge bypass

3d contours shaped to facial curves maintain a comfortable, continuous seal without pinching even when talking.
