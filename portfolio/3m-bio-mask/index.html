3m nexcare bio daily mask: competition concept

1st Place National Champion • 2nd Runner-Up ASEAN Regional • 3M Inspire Challenge 2021

context

competition: 3m inspire challenge 2021. team: team kancil, a student team from institut teknologi bandung (itb). result: 1st place national champion for indonesia, then 2nd runner-up across asean at the southeast asia regional finals. the submission was an academic competition proposal developed during industrial engineering studies.

problem [ the waste and the material ]
single-use ppe
the competition problem was the environmental surge of single-use personal protective equipment (ppe).
polypropylene that outlives us
standard surgical masks rely on petroleum-derived polypropylene plastic, a material that persists for centuries in landfills and waterways, from a petroleum supply chain.
approach [ material, structure, fit, end of life ]
phb, a grown biopolymer
the proposal explored polyhydroxybutyrate (phb), a biopolymer synthesized from cyanobacteria, grown on sugarcane waste and reinforced with kenaf, as a biodegradable non-woven alternative to fossil-based surgical mask fibers.
3-ply non-woven structure
the sheet was modelled as 3 plies: spunbond outer and inner plies around a meltblown filter.
fit that seals
3d facial contours were proposed to stop air leaks, with phb's memory shape as a roadmap feature for improved ergonomic fit without conventional plastic waste.
end of life
the material degrades safely in soil within 6 to 12 months, instead of persisting for centuries.
what was built [ the submission ]
the proposal
a material concept, a 3-ply structure model, and the techno-commercial structuring around them, developed as a student competition submission.
academic concept note
this project was an academic competition proposal developed during industrial engineering studies at institut teknologi bandung (itb). it represents early conceptual exploration and techno-commercial structuring, not a tested clinical medical device or commercially manufactured product.
outcome [ competition recognition ]
national champion
1st place national champion (indonesia) at the 3m inspire challenge 2021.
regional finals
advanced to the southeast asia regional finals, securing 2nd runner-up across asean participants.
