3M PHB Bio-Mask: Replacing Plastic Waste with Living Microbes

By Ramadhana Bhanuharya Krishnamurti • Award Winner • Open Presentation in Drive ↗

💡 The Problem in Plain English

During healthcare surges, billions of single-use surgical masks are thrown away daily. Standard masks are made from synthetic petroleum plastic (polypropylene meltblown fabrics) that take over 450 years to decompose in our oceans and landfills.

Instead of importing expensive petroleum plastics, our team formulated a 100% biodegradable mask made from Polyhydroxybutyrate (PHB)—a natural biopolymer created by harmless bacteria fermenting organic agricultural sugars right here in Indonesia. Once used, the mask naturally breaks down into harmless water and carbon dioxide within 90 days.

How the Sustainable Cycle Works

From local agricultural sugar waste to hospital-grade protection to natural soil compost.

STEP 01
Agricultural Feedstock
Sugarcane molasses and cassava starches harvested across Indonesian provinces.
STEP 02
Bacterial Fermentation
Bacteria feed on sugars and store energy as PHB biopolymer chains inside their cells.
STEP 03
Electrospun Fiber Mesh
PHB is spun into nanofiber layers with 98%+ bacterial filtration efficiency (BFE).
STEP 04
Zero-Waste Return
Decomposes in compost within 90 days without toxic microplastics or chemical residue.
