smart tailoring for everyday life: clothes for city walking

by ramadhana bhanuharya krishnamurti (krishna / naquuuu) • notes on style

the style thesis

clothing is everyday design. it's the layer between how our bodies feel and the city around us. approaching personal style through comfort, natural fabrics, relaxed proportions, and reliable footwear makes getting dressed simple, durable, and effortless.

1. clothes for moving through the city

walking through warm, humid cities like jakarta requires clothes that handle both air-conditioned offices and tropical heat outside.

the pieces that work best:

  • relaxed pleated trousers: pleats give your legs room to move and let air circulate naturally, staying comfortable through long days.
  • unstructured tailoring: soft, unlined jackets in wool, linen, or cotton that ditch stiff padding so they feel lightweight and pack easily.
  • shoes built for walking: classic leather brogues, comfortable loafers, and clean runners that look sharp while absorbing miles on city sidewalks.

2. keeping a simple personal uniform

having a wardrobe of well-fitting essentials reduces daily decision fatigue. when your pieces share a cohesive palette of navy, slate, charcoal, and warm neutrals, everything works together without thinking twice.
